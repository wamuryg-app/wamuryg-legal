Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Wamuryg is an on-device photo diary. Photos, reflections, emotion tags, albums, drafts, and Record Credits are stored in the app sandbox on the device you are using. Wamuryg does not operate an account server and does not upload your memories. Sample memories included with the app are copied onto the device only, and they are not a live social feed.

One-tap session

You open the app with One-Tap Login. There is no password and no Sign in with Apple. Log Out ends the session and leaves your memories on the device. Delete Account removes the on-device session together with memories, photos, drafts, albums, and Record Credits. Both actions are completed inside the app.

Camera, photos, and microphone

The camera is requested only after you tap Take Photo, to capture a picture for the memory you are saving. The photo library is requested when you tap Import from Photos. A separate add-only request is made when you tap Save to Photos on a memory or export card. The microphone and speech recognition are requested when you tap the voice button beside a reflection. Dictated words appear for you to insert; they are not kept until you do. If you deny a permission, you can still type, use the other photo path, and keep browsing memories already saved.

Notifications

After you enter the sanctuary, Wamuryg may ask to send notifications. The request uses the system dialog. A device token, when granted, stays on this device. Wamuryg does not ask to track you across other apps.

Record Credits

Saving a new memory uses one Record Credit after you confirm. Editing that same memory does not charge again. Credits are consumable, stay on this device, and are not restored if you delete the app or clear its data. Purchases are processed by Apple. Refunds are handled by Apple. If Apple revokes a purchase, only unused credits from that purchase are removed. Memories already saved stay.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent from Support inside Wamuryg.

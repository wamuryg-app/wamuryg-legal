User Agreement

Effective 19 October 2026

Wamuryg lets you keep a photo, a reflection, and emotion tags as one memory on your device, then look back by time, calendar, album, or feeling.

Your content

You decide what to photograph, import, write, and delete. A memory is written only after you confirm the cost on Save Review. Drafts can be resumed or discarded. Clear All Data removes memories, media, drafts, and remaining Record Credits from this installation.

Credits

New saves spend one Record Credit. A later edit of the same memory does not spend another. Packs are consumable in-app purchases sold by Apple at the price shown on the payment sheet. Credits are not a bank balance and cannot be restored after the app or its data is removed. Exporting a card to Photos does not spend a credit.

Account

One-Tap Login starts a session on this device. Log Out keeps your content. Delete Account erases the on-device account and the content tied to it. There is no password login and no Sign in with Apple.

Contact

Questions about this agreement can be sent from Support inside Wamuryg.
